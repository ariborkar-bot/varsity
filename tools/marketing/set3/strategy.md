## Rules for every video
- **Wording.** Say "tokens", "pick", "Over/Under", "locked in". Never say bet, gamble, cash, win money, payout, parlay. Every video that shows props or wagers ends with small print: **tokens only · no real money**.
- **End card (2.0-2.4 s, every video, styled to that video's look).** Big `VARSITY` wordmark, then `free · link in bio` (+ the small print where needed). It cuts in on a downbeat and holds still. No spinning logo.
- **Real names only.** Use them exactly as in `facts.json`. Never show the demo profile "Zed Hornby": rename the demo user (for example `you`) before recording. The owner was told that **paid ads** using minors' names need consent (a parent's for minors) in California. Posting organically is the owner's call. If any of these is boosted, swap in made-up names or get consent first.
- **Footage.** Record real app screens in true 9:16 (1080x1920, the `rec8.py` method) so they look like a phone screen recording, not a mockup. No AI images, no stock people, no fake UI.
- **Safe area.** Keep text between y = 250 and y = 1450 px and left of x = 940. TikTok's buttons and caption cover the bottom ~420 px and the right ~140 px.
- **Sound effects.** At most 2 per video, recorded or soft (a real tap, a page flick). No whooshes, airhorns or coin "cha-ching". Cuts should land on the music.
- **Captions.** On-screen text is lowercase and casual, 2-7 words per card, and stays up long enough to read twice.
- **Account type and music (decide before posting).** TikTok and Instagram **business** accounts are limited to the commercial music library. SocialPilot found most of this week's trending Reels sounds are likely restricted for business accounts ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-trends)). If Varsity posts from a business account, post with our original track, which becomes the "original sound" and can turn into its own trend, and pick the commercial-library backup. On a personal or creator account, add the recommended sound in-app at about 15-25% volume over our track, or replace our track with it.
- **Our music.** Every file ships with original synthesized music at the recommended sound's tempo, so cuts still land on the beat if the owner swaps the sound in-app. BPMs marked *(verify)* were not found in a source: tap-tempo the in-app sound before the final render and shift the cut times by the ratio.
