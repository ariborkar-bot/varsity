Computer skill
Each play: you and your opponent secretly pick a play. Offense: run, sweep, short pass or deep pass. Defense: blitz, run stop, zone or deep shell. Guess right and the play goes your way.
Then the timing meter: tap when the bar is in the middle. A good tap adds yards on offense, and on defense it makes tackles, sacks and picks more likely.
Drives: 4 downs for 10 yards. On 4th down you can punt or kick a field goal. Touchdown 7, field goal 3, safety 2. First to 21 wins.
