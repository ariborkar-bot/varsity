Varsity Gridiron Rush

Side view arcade football, 1 on 1. v5

Play against
Computer skill
One runs, one tackles. The ball carrier sprints down the field on his own. The defender is the only one in the way. Get 10 yards in 4 downs to keep the ball. Touchdown 7, first to 21.
Carrier: push the stick up or down to change lanes, hold BOOST for a speed burst (it uses stamina), and tap JUKE to dash sideways. A juke can't be tackled, so bait him in and dash past.
Defender: stick to chase him down, tap TACKLE to dive. A dive reaches far but if you miss you hit the grass.
Keys: arrows or WASD, Shift boost, Space juke / tackle.
