Varsity Gridiron Duel

First person receiver vs defender. v4

Computer skill
Receiver vs defender, first person. One of you is the receiver, the other covers him. A computer QB throws about 2 to 3 seconds after the snap, to where the receiver is heading. Catch it to gain yards, break it up or pick it off to win the snap. Get 10 yards in 4 downs to keep the ball. Touchdown 7, first to 21.
Receiver: run, then CUT to shake him. When BALL! shows your head snaps to it: tap CATCH when it is within reach. After the catch, JUKE past the tackler.
Defender: JAM the receiver at the line, stay on him, tap SWAT when the ball arrives (close = interception). After a catch, DIVE to tackle.
Controls: stick left/right turns, up runs, down backpedals. Keys: arrows or WASD, Shift sprint, Space action.
