Varsity Gridiron Rush

Run and pass mix. Pick a play, then play it. v6

Play against
Computer skill
Pick your play, then play it. Each snap the offense picks a run or a pass and the defense picks a call at the same time, then your CPU teammates run it. You control one player. Get 10 yards in 4 downs to keep the ball. Touchdown 7, first to 21.
Offense: you are the QB. Tap 1, 2 or 3 to throw to a receiver, or sidestep the rush. On a run or after a catch you control the ball carrier: pick a lane, BOOST and JUKE past tacklers.
Defense: you control the defender closest to the ball. Chase him down, SHED a block to rush the QB, SWAT passes and dive to TACKLE.
Keys: arrows or WASD, 1 2 3 to throw, Shift boost, Space action.
